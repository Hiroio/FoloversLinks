Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date you publish this]

1. Who this applies to

This Privacy Policy describes how Folovers ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Folovers iOS app. By creating an account, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in a few ways:

  • Account information. When you sign up, we receive an email address and, depending on how you sign in, basic profile details from Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In (such as your name or email, as permitted by that provider).
  • Profile & character. The display name, avatar appearance (hair, outfit, gender), and mood you choose inside the app.
  • Content you create. Folders, plans, photos you attach to a plan, letters you send to your connections, and todo items — including whichever of those you mark as visible to others.
  • Connections. Who you've connected with, and the status of pending requests.
  • Device & push notification data. A device token used to deliver notifications (for example, when someone sends you a letter), and basic app-usage data collected through Firebase Analytics.

3. How we use your information

  • To operate the core features of the app — folders, connections, letters, your calendar, and your character.
  • To deliver push notifications you've enabled.
  • To keep the app secure and working as intended, and to understand overall usage so we can improve it.

4. How your information is shared

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. Some information is visible to other users by design, because Folovers is a social app:

  • Your display name, avatar, and mood are visible to your connections.
  • Content inside a shared folder is visible to everyone who is a member of that folder.
  • Letters you send are visible to the recipient.
  • Todo items you mark public may be visible to your connections, unless you turn this off from your Privacy settings — in which case your list is hidden entirely, regardless of individual item settings.

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging, Analytics) as our backend infrastructure provider. Firebase processes data on our behalf under its own data processing terms.

5. Data storage & security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase's infrastructure and protected by access rules that restrict reads and writes to you and, where a feature is inherently social (folders, connections, letters), the specific other users that feature involves. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Your choices

  • You can control whether your todo list is visible to connections from Profile → Privacy.
  • You can control push notifications from Profile → Notification, or from your device's system settings.
  • You can disconnect from another user, or remove a letter, directly in the app.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at privacy@folovers.app.

7. Children's privacy

Folovers is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at privacy@folovers.app.
